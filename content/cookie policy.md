<p>At <strong>AURAE VENUIS</strong>, we believe visitors should have a clear understanding of how websites use cookies and similar tracking technologies. <h2>1. What Are Cookies?</h2>
<p>Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your computer, mobile phone, tablet, or other internet-connected device when you visit it. These files allow websites to recognize certain information about your browser or device.</p>
<p>For example, cookies can remember selected preferences, maintain website functionality, measure visitor activity, and help advertising services understand how advertisements perform. Depending on their purpose, cookies may be used for website functionality, analytics, advertising, security, or personalization.</p>
<p>Rest assured, a cookie does not normally give a website direct access to your personal files, photographs, or documents stored on your device.</p>

<h2>2. Why Do We Use Cookies?</h2>
<p>We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to maintain our website, understand visitor behavior, and improve the overall browsing experience. The information collected helps us understand which pages are useful, identify technical problems, and make our content easier to access.</p>
<p>Our website may use cookies for the following purposes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Website functionality:</strong> To support essential features and make sure pages operate correctly.</li>
<li><strong>User preferences:</strong> To remember certain settings and preferences where supported.</li>
<li><strong>Website analytics:</strong> To understand visitor activity, traffic sources, page views, and website performance.</li>
<li><strong>Advertising:</strong> To support the display and measurement of advertisements through services such as Google AdSense.</li>
<li><strong>Security:</strong> To help identify unusual activity, prevent misuse, and protect website functionality.</li>
<li><strong>Performance improvement:</strong> To understand how visitors use our website and identify areas that need improvement.</li>
</ul>

<h2>3. Types of Cookies Used on Our Website</h2>
<p>Not all cookies perform the same function. Some are required for basic operations, while others support analytics, advertising, and visitor preferences.</p>

<h3>Essential Cookies</h3>
<p>Sometimes called strictly necessary cookies, these support the basic operation and security of a website. They may help with page navigation, website functionality, security features, and the storage of cookie consent preferences. Without them, some website features may not operate correctly.</p>

<h3>Functional Cookies</h3>
<p>These help websites remember information that makes browsing more convenient, such as language preferences or selected settings, providing a more consistent experience when visitors return.</p>

<h3>Analytics Cookies</h3>
<p>These collect information about how visitors interact with a website. Depending on the analytics service, collected information may include pages visited, time spent on individual pages, browser and device information, general geographic location, referral sources, traffic channels, and engagement/interaction events.</p>

<h3>Advertising Cookies</h3>
<p>These support online advertising services. They may help advertising providers display advertisements, measure impressions, understand advertising performance, manage ad frequency, and detect invalid advertising activity. Depending on your consent choices, they may also be used to show personalized advertisements based on browsing activity.</p>

<h3>Session vs. Persistent Cookies</h3>
<p><strong>Session cookies</strong> are temporary files that generally remain active while your browser is open and are usually removed automatically when you close your browser. <strong>Persistent cookies</strong> remain on your device for a defined period or until you remove them manually, helping websites recognize returning browsers.</p>

This data is aggregated and anonymous, used purely to improve our website structure, content quality, and overall user experience.</p>

<h2>6. First-Party vs. Third-Party Cookies</h2>
<p><strong>First-party cookies</strong> are created by the website you are visiting (AURAE VENUIS). They support website functionality, remember preferences, and store information related to your interaction with the website.</p>
<p><strong>Third-party cookies</strong> are created by external services that operate on or through a website, such as advertising providers, analytics platforms, and embedded content providers. We do not independently control how external providers process information. Visitors should review the relevant third-party privacy policies for further details.</p>

<h2>7. How to Manage Your Cookie Settings</h2>
<p>You are in control of your cookie preferences. Most modern web browsers allow users to control cookie settings through their privacy and security preferences. Depending on your browser, you may be able to view cookies stored on your device, delete existing cookies, block cookies from selected websites, restrict third-party cookies, or receive notifications when websites attempt to store cookies.</p>
<p>Keep in mind that blocking all cookies may affect certain website features. Some pages or services may not function properly if necessary cookies are disabled.</p>
