<p>At <strong>AURAE VENUIS</strong>, we believe in being completely honest and transparent with our readers. When we recommend a product, service, or resource, we want you to understand whether we may receive compensation if you decide to make a purchase.</p>

<p>To put it simply: some of the links published on this website are <strong>affiliate links</strong>. This means we may earn a small commission when you click a link and complete a <strong>qualifying purchase</strong> through the linked website. This comes at <strong>no extra cost to you</strong>.</p>

<p>This Affiliate Disclosure explains how these links work, how our website earns commissions, and what this means for you as a visitor.</p>

<h2>1. What Exactly Are Affiliate Links?</h2>
<p>Affiliate links are special <strong>tracking links</strong> that allow a retailer to identify when a visitor reaches their website through a referral from another site.</p>
<p>For example, if we recommend a beautiful lamp and include an affiliate link to an online store, the retailer may compensate us if you purchase that product through our link. The commission is paid by the participating company or affiliate network as part of their marketing arrangement. </p>
<p>You are never obligated to use our links. You are always free to visit the retailer's website directly and make your purchase there.</p>

<h2>2. How Do We Earn Commissions?</h2>
<p>We participate in various <strong>affiliate marketing programs</strong> offered by retailers, brands, and affiliate networks. These programs allow publishers to earn <strong>referral commissions</strong> by directing interested visitors to their products.</p>
<p>The process is straightforward:</p>
<ul>
<li>We publish an article or product review that may include an affiliate link.</li>
<li>You click the link and visit the retailer's website.</li>
<li>If you complete an eligible purchase, the affiliate provider records the referral.</li>
<li>We may receive a small commission for that qualifying action.</li>
</ul>
<p>Not every click results in a commission, and not every product mentioned on our website is an affiliate product.</p>

<h2>3. Amazon Associates Program</h2>
<p>AURAE VENUIS may participate in the <strong>Amazon Services LLC Associates Program</strong>, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide websites with a way to earn advertising fees by linking to Amazon.com and affiliated websites.</p>
<p>As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. Please note that Amazon has its own pricing, product availability, shipping policies, and return conditions. We do not control these matters. Always review the final price and terms directly on Amazon before making a purchase.</p>

<h2>4. Will Affiliate Links Cost You More?</h2>
<p><strong>Absolutely not.</strong> In standard affiliate arrangements, using an affiliate link does not add an extra charge to the price you pay.</p>
<p>The commission is paid by the retailer as part of their marketing budget, not by you. You receive the exact same product under the retailer's normal pricing and purchasing conditions.</p>
<p>We include affiliate links as a way to support the time and effort it takes to maintain our website and publish free, useful content for you.</p>

<h2>5. Our Editorial Integrity and Product Reviews</h2>
<p>We know that you rely on our reviews and recommendations when deciding what to buy. That is why we aim to make our <strong>editorial content</strong> useful, relevant, and fair.</p>
<p>Our recommendations focus on factors that actually matter:</p>
<ul>
<li>Product features and specifications.</li>
<li>Quality, durability, and overall usefulness.</li>
<li>Price and value for money.</li>
<li>Advantages, disadvantages, and available alternatives.</li>
</ul>
<p>An affiliate relationship does <strong>not</strong> automatically make a product our preferred recommendation. If a product has limitations or is not suitable for certain users, we believe you should have access to that information. Our priority is maintaining your trust, not just making a sale.</p>

<p>In addition to affiliate links, we may occasionally publish <strong>sponsored content</strong> or paid promotions. </p>
<p>Sponsored content is different from normal affiliate content because a brand may pay us directly to feature or promote specific material. Whenever this happens, we will clearly identify it as sponsored so you can distinguish it from our independently published editorial material. The presence of a sponsored article does not mean every article on our site is paid.</p>

<h2>7. Display Advertising and Google AdSense</h2>
<p>Our website also displays advertisements through <strong>Google AdSense</strong> and other advertising networks.</p>
<p>It's important to know the difference: <strong>display advertising</strong> is separate from affiliate marketing. With display advertising, we earn revenue simply when ads are displayed or interacted with. With affiliate marketing, we only earn a commission if you actually make a qualifying purchase through a referral link.</p>
<p>Both methods help keep AURAE VENUIS running. For more information about how advertising affects your data, please read our <a href="/privacy/">Privacy Policy</a> and <a href="/cookies/">Cookie Policy</a>.</p>
